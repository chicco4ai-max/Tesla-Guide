HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Inspect. Record. Confirm.

  • Mark each check as Good, Problem, or Later.
  • If there is a problem, write exactly where and what you found.
  • Photograph defects using your phone camera. Keep the originals with their dates.
  • Review significant issues with a Tesla advisor and request written acknowledgment.

Safety first: Do not override safety alerts, inspect high-voltage components, or perform workshop-only tests.
